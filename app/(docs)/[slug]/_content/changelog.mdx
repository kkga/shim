## 29 November 2025

### Theme

* **Breaking:** Refactored theme structure to use nested `variants` object instead of flat `fieldVariant` and `buttonVariant` props;
* Added `table` and `badge` variant types to theme context;
* Added `buildVariantOverrides()` helper for consistent variant override construction across components;
* Updated all components to use standardized theme merging pattern;
* Updated [theming documentation](/theming) with new API and examples;

***

## 28 November 2025

### Badge

* Added `solid` and `soft` [variants](/components/badge#badge-variant) (in addition to the existing `surface` variant);

***

## 27 November 2025

### Disclosure and DisclosureGroup

* Added `soft` and `ghost` [variants](/components/disclosure-group#disclosure-group-variant) (in addition to the existing `surface` variant);
* Refactored `Disclosure` anatomy by removing the `DisclosureHeader` and `DisclosurePanel` subcomponents, replaced with `title` and `children` props;

***

## 26 November 2025

### Table

* Added `ghost` and `zebra` [variants](/components/table#table-variant) (in addition to the existing `surface` variant);
* Added [selection example](/components/table#table-selection);
* Added support for `size` prop and [size example](/components/table#table-size);

### Tooltip

* Added support for `size` prop and [size example](/components/tooltip#tooltip-size);
